# Fitness Buddy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Fitness Buddy is a calorie, nutrition and workout tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device

Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goals), food diary, workouts, weigh-ins, water and steps are stored only on your device. There is no account and no Fitness Buddy server that stores your diary. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Data sent to other services

- **Food databases.** When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts and the USDA FoodData Central database to look up nutrition facts. No personal information is attached.
- **AI features.** When you use Snap a meal or the Coach, the photo or message you send, plus a short summary of your goals and today's log, is sent to Anthropic's Claude API through our relay server to generate the answer. The relay does not store the content. Anthropic's handling of API data is described at <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy>.
- **Step count** is read from your phone's motion sensors and stays on the device.

We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Your choices

AI features only run when you use them. You can reset all data from Profile → Erase all data, or delete the app.

## Children

Fitness Buddy is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/JonathanxJarvis/fitness-buddy/issues>.

## App Store "App Privacy" answers (for the developer)

- **Data collected:** Yes, because photos and messages sent to the AI leave the device.
  - _User Content → Photos or Videos_ and _Other User Content_: used for **App Functionality**, **not linked** to the user's identity, **not used for tracking**.
  - _Health & Fitness → Fitness_ (the goals/log summary sent with AI requests): App Functionality, not linked, not used for tracking.
- **Tracking:** No.
